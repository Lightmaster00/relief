Every app, sounding its best.

Relief works on all the sound of your Mac: browsers, music and video apps, games, calls.

3D surround

A wide, deep stereo image, tuned separately for speakers and for headphones.

Equalizer

Ten bands, sixteen presets and your own, with a live frequency-response curve.

Volume booster

Up to +12 dB for quiet videos and small speakers, with a limiter that keeps it clean.

Effects

Ambience, clarity, bass boost, and a night mode that evens out loud and quiet passages.

Per-app volume

Turn the music down without touching your call. Each app keeps its own level.

Follows your output

AirPods, speakers, an external DAC: Relief switches with you, at any sample rate.

Light, native, private.

Relief uses the audio capture built into macOS — no driver to install, nothing to configure. The sound is processed in real time on your Mac and never recorded. Turn Relief off, and your Mac plays its normal sound.

Native

Written in Swift on Core Audio. It starts instantly and stays in the menu bar.

Free

Free and open source (MIT license). No account, no subscription, no tracking.

Always up to date

New versions are offered automatically and install in one click.
